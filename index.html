Musings on Game Dev

You can go here to find the first article about my process of developing the game or you can check out the categories or tags pages to narrow down what you're looking for. Hopefully my mistakes and frustrations will help you figure out your own processes.

I may also post here from time to time about some of my other interests including travel, gaming, urbanism, socialism and trains.
